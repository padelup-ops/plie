Origamo

24 games for iPhone Duo

In short

Origamo collects no data. No account, no ads, no usage analytics, no third-party services, and the app sends nothing over the internet.

Camera and photos

Some games use the camera (to spot who moves or take pictures of the players). Analysis runs on the iPhone; photos stay in the app for the game only and are never sent anywhere.

Your games

Your progress, records and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases them.

Purchase

Buying the app is handled by Apple. We receive no payment information.
