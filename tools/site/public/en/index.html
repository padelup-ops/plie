Origamo

24 games played with the hinge and both screens of iPhone Duo: close it, open it, fold it into an L or lay it flat. On other iPhone models, your finger stands in for the hinge.

FAQ

How do I play a game? Every game has a “?” button with an illustrated guide.

Is everything included? Yes: one purchase, every game, every level and every mini-duel. No subscription, no ads.

On another iPhone? Download Origamo with the same Apple Account, no need to pay again.

Why the camera? In Red Light, Green Light, Draw It, Shell Game and Forbidden Words, to spot who moves or take pictures of the players. Everything stays on the iPhone.
