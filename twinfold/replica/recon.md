# Recon: two-pane browsers for iPhone Duo (Replica pack, clean-room)

Studied from public sources only: the App Store listing, the developer's public site and guide, public posts.
Nothing was decompiled, no code or assets were copied. Twinfold has its own name, icon, palette, words and code.

Core loop: open two pages at once, one per half of the unfolded Duo (or stacked on any iPhone), and act on
the active one from a single bar.

Out of scope for 1.0: AI side panel, RSS follow, profiles/workspaces, iCloud sync, custom fonts, iPad.
Angle: available in the EU, free, private (no server, no analytics), designed around the fold.
