Country sourdough: shaping and scoring

Follow along with episode 3 · 12 min read
2loaves
75%water
24 htotal

Ingredients

  • 800 g bread flour
  • 200 g whole wheat flour
  • 750 g water, lukewarm
  • 200 g active starter
  • 20 g fine sea salt

Shaping

  1. Tip the dough onto an unfloured counter and divide it in two.
  2. Pre-shape each half into a loose round and rest it, uncovered, for 25 minutes.
  3. Flour the top, flip it, and fold the bottom third up, then the sides in.
  4. Roll it toward you to build tension, and set it seam side up in a floured basket.
Watching the video in the other pane? Pause at 7:40: that is the fold to copy.

Scoring

  1. Turn the cold loaf onto parchment.
  2. Hold the blade at 30° and make one confident cut, about 1 cm deep.
