The old ferry pier reopens as a market hall this spring

After three years of work, the pier's warehouse will host forty stalls, a bakery and a rooftop terrace looking out over the bay.

Opening hours run from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and the ferry to the islands returns to its original berth next month.
