The 8 steps, in order

Everything happens on the device, iPhone or iPad. Tick each step once it's done: the page remembers it on this device.

0 / 8 steps done

  1. 1Get your device's identifier2 min

    The app isn't on the App Store: it is signed for one specific device, iPhone or iPad. So you need its identifier, the UDID, a 25-character string that looks like 00008160-000A1B2C3D4E5F6A.

    1. In Safari, open a service that reads the UDID. It offers to install a small configuration profile.
    2. Approve the profile in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management. The page then shows your UDID.
    3. Copy the identifier, then delete the profile in the same place.
    4. Send the identifier to have your build signed. You get two install links back.

    The identifier passes through the service you use. Pick one you trust.

  2. 2Install the two apps3 min

    You received two links: one for StikDebug, which gives the emulator the right to translate game code on the fly (JIT), and one for the emulator, MeloNX.

    1. Copy the first link in full and paste it into Safari's address bar.
    2. Tap Open, then Install. The icon appears on the Home Screen.
    3. Do the same with the second link.

    The links expire after about ten minutes. If iOS answers “Unable to Install”, ask for new ones.

  3. 3Turn on Developer Mode2 min + restart

    iOS refuses to open an app signed this way while this mode is off.

    1. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode. The row appears once the apps are installed.
    2. Turn the switch on, then tap Restart.
    3. When the device is back, confirm with Turn On and enter your passcode.
    Developer Mode
  4. 4Install LocalDevVPN1 min

    It's a free app on the App Store. It doesn't send your traffic anywhere: it connects the device to itself, so StikDebug can talk to it.

    1. Install LocalDevVPN from the App Store.
    2. Open it, tap Connect and allow the VPN configuration.

    Reconnect it before every play session.

  5. 5Pair StikDebug with your device3 min

    On iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 or later, pairing happens entirely on the device. Before that, it took a computer.

    1. Open StikDebug. The “Pairing file unavailable” alert is normal at this point: tap Cancel.
    2. Tap the gear, then Pair a Device › Pair iPhone or iPad. A six-digit code appears.
    3. Without closing StikDebug, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode and tap the Pair with … row.
    4. Enter the code. Back in StikDebug, you read “Pairing Complete”.
    482913

    No “Pair with …” row? StikDebug must stay open on its pairing screen while you switch to Settings.

  6. 6Enable JIT and open the emulator1 min, every launch

    This is the move to repeat every time you want to play.

    1. Check that LocalDevVPN is connected.
    2. In StikDebug, tap MeloNX in the “Enable JIT” list. The emulator opens with JIT on.
    MeloNXJIT on

    Always open the emulator from StikDebug, not from the Home Screen. If it freezes, turn on “Always Run Scripts” and “Silent Audio” in StikDebug's settings.

  7. 7Import your keys and firmware5 min

    The emulator needs two files dumped from your own console. This guide provides neither.

    1. On first launch, the “Setup” screen appears. Tap Import Keys and pick your prod.keys file in Files.
    2. Tap Add Firmware and pick the .zip archive of your firmware.
    3. Keep the app open for a minute while the firmware installs.

    The firmware must not be newer than your keys, or the import fails. The simplest way: dump both on the same day, from the same console.

  8. 8Add your games2 min

    The emulator reads copies of your own games, in .nsp and .xci formats.

    1. In the library, tap + (“Add Game”) and pick your files.
    2. Or drop them into Files › On My iPhone (or On My iPad) › MeloNX › roms.
    3. Tap a game to launch it.

    A game's first launch takes longer than the next ones. Not every title runs: it depends on the game and on your device's memory.

Troubleshooting

The errors we actually ran into

“Pairing file unavailable”

Nothing is broken: StikDebug is only saying that pairing hasn't been done yet. Go back to step 5.

“Failed to connect debug proxy: ServiceNotFound”

StikDebug reached the device but can't find the debugging service on it. Check that LocalDevVPN is connected and that you have Internet. In StikDebug, tap the gear, then “Redownload DDI”, and wait for “DDI files refreshed successfully”. Quit StikDebug completely, reopen it, try again. If the error comes back, restart the device. On a brand-new iOS version, you sometimes have to wait for a StikDebug update.

The screen goes black after the library

The emulator is waiting for an answer from StikDebug. Turn on “Always Run Scripts” and “Silent Audio” in StikDebug's settings, then enable JIT again. On a foldable iPhone, make sure you have the latest build of the app: an older one left the large screen black once the phone was unfolded.

The app closes as soon as it opens

Check that Developer Mode is still on (step 3). If it is, reinstall the latest build with new links.

The firmware won't import

Import the keys first, then the firmware. The file name must end in .zip exactly. If the message mentions a missing key, your firmware is newer than your keys: redo both dumps on the same day.

“Unable to Install” when opening a link

The link has expired (about ten minutes) or the device isn't the one the app was signed for. Ask for new links and check the identifier you sent in step 1.

The app stops opening after several months

The signature is valid for one year. After that, you need to reinstall a freshly signed build. Your keys, firmware and saves stay in the app as long as you don't delete it.
