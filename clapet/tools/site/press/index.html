What it does

  • Reads the hinge live: the angle is shown as a gauge, and a fold is counted when the Duo closes below 12°.
  • Counts all day, even when the app is closed, with Shortcuts' "When iPhone is Closed / Opened" triggers. The two automations install in one tap.
  • A daily limit, with alerts at 80 % and 100 %, and how many folds you have left.
  • Time open: how long the Duo stays open each day.
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, like the steps in Health.
  • History: 30 days, 12 months, streaks under the limit, records, and your folds by weekday and hour. CSV export.
  • Two themes: Classic (cards and a ring, like Health) or Modern (big numbers, hinge gauge, Today and Week tabs).
