Santa calls your child

In under a minute, prepare a magical, personal call from Santa: your child answers, and Santa talks to them by name, mentions what makes them special, gives them time to answer and ends with a surprise.

The voice sounds robotic?

Santa's voice is recorded in the app. Only the elf, who says what you typed, uses a voice made by the iPhone: for a warmer one, download a free "Enhanced" or "Premium" voice: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.

I can't hear anything

Turn the iPhone's volume up. The call plays even in silent mode.

I bought the full magic on another iPhone

Open Settings in the app, then "Restore purchases".
