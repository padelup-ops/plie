RetroFold

A 90s handheld console for iPhone Duo

How to play

  1. Closed: pick a cartridge on the cover screen (swipe through them).
  2. Unfold your iPhone Duo like a clamshell: the screen is above the fold, the controls below.
  3. Fold it again to pause. Unfold to resume.
