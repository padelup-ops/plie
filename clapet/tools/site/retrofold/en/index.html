Support

How do I play?

Closed: pick a cartridge on the cover screen. Unfold your iPhone Duo like a clamshell: the screen is above the fold, the controls below. Fold it again to pause, unfold to resume.

How does the free day work?

Open the store (the shopping bag) and tap Start under “Free for 1 day”. For 24 hours, everything is unlocked. After that, the cartridges ask for RetroFold Pro.

How do I get RetroFold Pro back on another iPhone?

Open the store and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does RetroFold collect anything?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics. Your scores, achievements and settings stay on your iPhone. Privacy
