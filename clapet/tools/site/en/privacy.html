Foldly

The fold counter for foldable phones

In short

Foldly collects no data. No account, no ads, no usage analytics, no third-party services.

What the app stores

The number of folds and openings (per day and per hour), your daily limit and your settings. These numbers stay on your iPhone: they are shared only with the app's widgets, in the device keychain. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Notifications and Shortcuts

Limit alerts are local notifications created on the iPhone. Foldly' Shortcuts actions run on the device.

Purchase

Buying the app is handled by Apple. We receive no payment information.

Delete your data

In the app: Settings → Reset all folds. Deleting the app also deletes its data.
