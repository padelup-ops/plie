Privacy policy

The short version

The app has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress, pins, notes and photo stay on your devices.

What stays on your device

Missions and objectives you tick, places you visit, collectible counters, pins, character notes, your name and photo, and the chapter you're in. With Pro, they're copied to your own iCloud so your devices stay in sync; we can't see them.

What the app downloads

New guide content from our server (a public file; nothing about you is sent). Music previews come from Apple's public iTunes Search service.

PlayStation (optional, Pro)

If you connect PlayStation, you sign in on Sony's own page. The app never sees your password. It keeps a sign-in token in this device's keychain and only reads your list of games and play time. Disconnect at any time in Profile.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions go through Apple. We don't receive your payment details.
